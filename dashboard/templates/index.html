How to read this

Pullback (green) — strong rank + healthy pullback + RSI not overbought: best long setups.

Extended (yellow) — leading but stretched: near its 10-day high or RSI overbought.

Fade (red) — weak rank + elevated RSI or near highs: potential short-term short.

Consolidate (gray) — everything else: sideways, low conviction.

% from 10d High = pullback depth. RSI14 = 14-period RSI. Rel Vol (10d) = today's volume ÷ 10-day average volume.

Universe = broad market beta (SPY, QQQ, IWM) + the 11 SPDR sector ETFs + high-volume thematics (SMH, KRE, XBI) — the highest-volume, most institutionally-relevant vehicles, not individual stocks.

Category cutoffs (rank thirds, RSI 70/30, pullback bands) are inferred defaults, not verified exact thresholds — tune them in rotation_screener.py if needed.

About this data

Congressional trade disclosures (STOCK Act) only require a dollar range per trade — never an exact price or share count. There is no "shares bought" field in this data by law, only the disclosed bracket (e.g. "$1,001 – $15,000").

Party affiliation isn't part of the disclosure either — it's resolved separately against the public unitedstates/congress-legislators dataset. An unresolved or ambiguous name shows as Unknown rather than a guess.

Filed is when the trade became public, not when it happened — Lag is the gap between the two, and can legally run 30–45 days.
